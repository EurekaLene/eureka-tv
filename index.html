01First impressions

Customers judge us before the work starts.
  • Reply fast, even if it is only to say when you will have an answer
  • Answer the phone with the business name and your name
  • Arrive on time, prepared and presentable
  • Quotes show scope, exclusions and validity, not just a price

02Listening and questions

Understand the problem before offering a fix.
  • Ask before you recommend
  • Find the real problem behind the request
  • Repeat back what you heard to confirm it
  • Work out who decides, who uses it and who pays

03Setting expectations

Most complaints come from a gap between what was expected and what was delivered.
  • Agree in writing what “done” looks like
  • Give timing news early, good or bad
  • Say plainly what the system will not do
  • Explain ongoing maintenance and running costs up front

04Explaining technical things

If they do not understand it, they will not buy it or trust it.
  • Match the detail to the person in front of you
  • Swap jargon for everyday words
  • Offer two or three options with a clear recommendation
  • Leave them able to check the system themselves

05Price and value

Customers compare price. Help them compare value.
  • Talk cost over the life of the system, not just upfront
  • Explain what is different about a cheaper quote
  • Adjust scope before you cut price
  • Flag variations before doing the extra work

06Difficult moments

How we handle problems matters more than whether we have them.
  • Listen first, fix second, blame never
  • Own our mistakes quickly and say how we will fix them
  • Hold the line on safety and compliance
  • Know when and who to escalate to

07On site

Every site visit is a reference check.
  • Follow site rules: sign in, PPE, permits
  • Tell operators before you isolate anything
  • Leave the area cleaner than you found it
  • Report problems you notice, even outside your job

08Follow-up and loyalty

Keeping a customer costs less than winning a new one.
  • Check in after every job
  • Offer scheduled servicing, not just breakdown callouts
  • Record customer and site history where everyone can find it
  • Notice when a regular customer goes quiet
